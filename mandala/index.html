About the atlas

A map in progress

This interactive study follows the Karma Lingpa Zhitro mandala image hosted by Tsadra. The working orientation is east at the bottom.

The concentric enclosures lead inward through fire, the vajra fence, blue water and wind ribbons, eight charnel-ground vignettes and lotus petals to the four-gated palace and its central sanctuary. The drawing follows the supplied painting; the repeated charnel-ground vignettes are symbolic and do not identify a particular named set.

Toggle the peaceful and wrathful assemblies independently, or choose Palace only to study the architecture. Shared family and gate locations remain visible when either relevant assembly is on. Their individual painted marks have not been assigned to one assembly. The original painting is an unchanged reference, so its painted marks cannot be hidden.

The atlas includes all 100 catalog identities. Its 28 courtyard positions are a schematic working arrangement. Locations are compared with Tsadra ritual texts and BardoOS. No individual placement has been verified against a key to this painting.
